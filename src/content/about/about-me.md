Hi, I'm Julian Vane, a visual artist and documentary photographer based in Bath, United Kingdom. With over a decade of experience behind the lens, I capture the raw beauty of natural landscapes, the subtle emotions of editorial portraits, and the fast-paced stories of urban street life.

My philosophy is simple: chase the natural light and capture the silent details. I believe that every photograph should tell a story and evoke a memory.

### Exhibitions & Features:
- **Solo Exhibition**: "Silent Ridges", Bath Art Center, 2024
- **Featured in**: British Journal of Photography, Fine Art Photo Magazine
- **Awards**: UK Landscape Photographer of the Year (Finalist, 2023)
