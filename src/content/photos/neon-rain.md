Shot during a heavy downpour in central London. The wet asphalt became a mirror reflecting the bright yellow of passing cabs and the warm glow of storefront neon signs. I waited under a bus shelter for almost an hour to capture the perfect moment of movement and reflection.

### Technique Details:
- **Exposure**: 1/125s at f/1.4, ISO 800
- **Post-processing**: Enhanced color contrast between blue shadows and warm yellow highlights.
