Captured at sunrise in the heart of the Dolomites. The valley was covered in a thick blanket of morning fog, with only the highest peaks piercing through the clouds. The soft golden light hit the rock faces just as I clicked the shutter, creating a strong contrast between the rugged textures and the soft mist.

### Technique Details:
- **Exposure**: 1/60s at f/8.0, ISO 100
- **Focal Length**: 45mm
- **Filters**: Medium ND Grad to balance the bright sky with the dark valleys.
