An editorial studio portrait focusing on light, shadow, and minimalist forms. Using a single softbox positioned to the side, I wanted to shape the light around the model's profile to create a sculpture-like representation of beauty. The skin tones were kept warm and natural against a clean sand-colored backdrop.

### Technique Details:
- **Exposure**: 1/160s at f/1.8, ISO 100
- **Lighting**: Profoto D2 with a 3ft Octabox.
